## FlyDSL startup precompile (crsuse2-m2m-255 2026-10-08, sglang working tree, uncommitted)

every M of its padded-M range. aiter dsv41 table: 68 M rows per shape map to 29-51 distinct kernels (723 pairs over
18 shapes; ~300 for the TP4 shapes). mxfp8_aiter_gfx95.precompile_preshuffled_mxfp8(n, k): once per process and shape,
compile-only (aiter compile_only_env + compile_bmm_a8w8_mxfp8_gfx950) every kernel aiter can pick -- the tuned rows'
kernelNames, or for untuned shapes pick_bmm_kernel_name at M 1..32768 (no per-M warning spam) -- under a /tmp file lock
so a node's TP ranks take turns. Called from fp8_hip.process_dense_weights after the preshuffle and from the DSpark
row-stacked wkv build. Fresh-cache check (FLYDSL_RUNTIME_CACHE_DIR=/tmp/...): first call 1792x5120 M=5000 1075 -> 15.6 ms,
M=7 270 -> 14.9 ms (disk-cache load remains ~15 ms per new kernel); precompile 1792x5120 (47) + 1280x5120 (11) = 31.8 s
fresh, ~0.4-1.1 s per shape when cached. E2E check running: chain_precomp.sh (PGID 346613), bmm cache dirs moved to
