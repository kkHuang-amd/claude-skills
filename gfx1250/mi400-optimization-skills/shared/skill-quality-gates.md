# Skill Quality Gates

A skill run is complete only when:
1. Routing evidence shows why the skill triggered.
2. Baseline and correctness are reproducible.
3. The agent cites the decision rule used.
4. At least one plausible alternative explanation is tested or ruled out.
5. Change is isolated enough to attribute the result.
6. Required anti-pattern review is recorded.
7. The optimization checklist is completed.
8. Results include representative workloads and regressions.
9. Unsupported claims are labeled unknown rather than guessed.
