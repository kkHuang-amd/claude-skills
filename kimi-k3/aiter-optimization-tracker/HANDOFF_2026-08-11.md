### HIP TopK + MXFP8 quant producer

A gfx950-native producer was implemented inside the existing one-wave AITER
TopK block. It produced bit-identical IDs, weights, FP8 payload, and E8M0
scales, including graph replay and strided K3 router inputs.

It failed the first performance gate:


Root cause: quant becomes serialized behind routing in the one-wave block,
while the current standalone quant uses many blocks/CUs. Decision: remove the
producer and skip runner/stage1/endpoint work.

### V4 persistent route-prep prototype

V4 removed the HBM expert mesh and implemented count, padded prefix, stable
scatter, and standard AITER sorted outputs in one persistent FlyDSL launch.
Random legal TopK inputs matched Opus for block_m 32/64.

The design failed its first gate. Opus graph sort measured about 14.6 us while
the persistent kernel measured 226-339 us after limiting the grid to active
route blocks. The serialized 896-expert prefix and coherent cross-CU
publication dominated; replacing atomics with plain stores broke visibility.

Decision: remove V4 and skip producer, chain, serving, and production work.
Do not use multi-CU persistent barriers for B1-B32 route prep. Future work must
either use a one-CTA LDS E896 sorter or change stage1 to consume route metadata
without rebuilding the sorted ABI.

Details:
[`V4_PERSISTENT_ROUTE_PREP_2026-08-11.md`](V4_PERSISTENT_ROUTE_PREP_2026-08-11.md).
