## Summary

Kimi-K3 is already enabled on AITER main through SiTUv2 two-stage MoE,
strided grouped-topk, BF16/A8W8/A8W4/A4W4/FP4 tuned configurations, gfx1250
routing, and Opus sorted-intermediate support. The largest open optimization
areas are:

1. small-head and long-context MLA decode;
2. fused KDA, MLA-gate, pre-route, and latent-MoE projections;
3. A4W4/SiTUv2 MoE tuning;
4. CUDA-graph-safe split-K and reusable graph workspaces;
5. gfx942 and gfx1250 coverage.
