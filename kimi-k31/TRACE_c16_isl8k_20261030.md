## Principles pass on VERIFY (`scripts/trace_principles.py`, `scripts/trace_layer_seq.py`)

- P1 per-layer -> per-step: DSA/MLA layers run 25 kernels each (7.58 ms/step); KDA layers 5 extra
  (4.86 ms). Layer-invariant candidates to audit in a DSA layer: `dcp_global_slots` 22.7 us,
  `dcp_local_candidates`, `_localize_compact`, `_bf16_head_gate_q_scale`, and 12 identical
  `at::native::vectorized_elementwise_kernel` (~3.8 us each, 2 bursts of 6) around the indexer.
- P2 host sync: one `hipEventSynchronize` per step (CPU waits 44.9 ms) but GPU idle between steps
  is ~0.06 ms; post-verify region is 0.95 ms of mostly GPU work (replayssm fold 0.33 ms). Low priority.
- P3 small kernels: 2581 kernels/VERIFY step; 1452 under 10 us = 7.72 ms (16%); 779 under 5 us.
  Graph replay shows no gaps, but sub-5 us kernels are ramp/latency dominated. Fusion chains seen
  per layer: AR -> add_rmsnorm_quant -> GEMM -> add3 -> _agg (attn_res); situ_and_mul between two GEMMs;
  topk_reg -> 2x moe_sorting -> fused_mx_quant_moe_sort (4 launches before MoE stage1).
- P4 independence: single stream for the whole step (sum kernel time / span = 1.000).
  Overlap candidates: comm (xGMI) vs HBM-bound compute; DSA indexer branch vs MLA q/kv path;
  shared experts vs routed MoE prologue.

## Candidate optimizations (ranked by expected endpoint impact)

1. Prefill NCCL all-reduce, 23% of prefill, serialized with compute. Options: comm/compute overlap
   (two-batch overlap in prefill), reduce-scatter + all-gather around norms, larger custom-AR
   coverage / quick-reduce at bf16 (precision floor forbids quantized AR).
2. Prefill sparse MLA (Triton, 5.2 ms/layer). Check aiter OPUS sparse prefill PRs (#7000 fp8
   warp-specialized H=128, #6927 bf16 sparse prefill H>32) for K3.1 head count / fp8_ds_mla cache.
3. Decode bf16 GEMMs 10.9 ms/step at M=128. Need shapes (one capture with record_shapes) to compute
   achieved bandwidth vs roofline; candidates: aiter GEMM table PRs #7169 / #6959, verify-size rows.
4. Decode comm 6.3 ms: 187 custom-AR calls/step (2/layer). Fusing AR + residual + RMSNorm
   (aiter #6270) or AG-lastdim-add (#6960) removes the separate norm/add kernels.
5. KDA decode 2.5 ms: opt-in fused backend `SGLANG_ROCM_K3_KDA_FUSED_BACKEND=aiter` (aiter #6026) — cheap A/B.
6. attn_res `_agg_kernel` 191 launches/step (1.6 ms decode, 61 ms prefill): fusion candidate.
7. Scheduling: decode waits for whole-batch prefill (c8/c16 TPOT inflation) — chunked-prefill /
   mixed-batch policy, cf. DEV_AMD "c8's P90 set by prefill turns stalling decode".
