- Lowest p10 clock seen = ~1017 MHz (c=16 GPU6) -> a safe lock is <= ~1000 MHz.

AR split (`ar_floor.py`, pattern `nccl|ar_ll|all_reduce|allreduce|cross_device`, 25 AR/step):

| c | own (min over ranks) median us/call | mean over ranks median us/call | wait share | wait ms/step |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 16 | 27.2 | 109.1 | 76% | ~6.7 |
| 64 | 48.5 | 250.9 | 85% | ~6.4 of 30 ms |

- Per-rank non-AR totals differ only ~4% (c=64: TP0/1 434 ms vs TP2-7 ~451 ms over 20 steps), so the wait is per-call jitter, not a slow rank.
- Wait = cross-rank kernel-time variation, not GPU idle (`ar_segment_skew.py`; pattern MUST be case-insensitive `(?i)allreduce|ar_ll`,
  c=64 ARs are RCCL DDA `dda::common::ddaAllReduceTwoShotLL`): c=64 busy max-mean 15.33 vs span 15.44 ms/step; c=16 3.86 vs 4.56.
  Largest spread c=64: gate-up a8w4 K16384 1.87 ms/step, down 0.89, a16w16 0.60.
- CORRECTION: `ar_segment_skew.py` matches ARs by call index; ranks captured 499 vs 500 ARs, so its segments are shifted
  (c=64 "15.33 ms" is wrong). Re-done with end-time alignment, `scripts/ar_align_skew.py` (496/499 aligned, end spread 9 us):
  c=64 busy per step max-mean 6.42 / mean-min 6.22 / max-min 12.64 ms; arrival max-mean 6.38 (= ar_floor wait 6.4). c=16: busy 5.28/4.05/9.33.
  Per-rank mean offsets are small (c=64 TP0/1 -30 us per segment, rest +5..+13: socket 0 fastest, matches its higher clock); the spread is
  per-instance. Last arrival is usually the odd partition of whichever socket is slow (c=64 last: TP1 108, TP7 99, TP5 82, TP3 74 of 496).
- Socket signature (`scripts/rank_corr.py`, gate-up per-call durations): c=64 same-socket pairs 1.00/1.00/1.00/0.65,
  cross-socket median 0.16 (0.00-0.40); TP0 p10/p50/p90 463/848/1354 us. Same as 10-23 -> per-socket dynamic state (PPT DPM) drives the wait.
  c=16 cross-socket 0.93: that window mixes prefill + decode (batch-size changes hit all ranks), so not informative there.
- Conclusion: at c=64 ~6.4 ms of a 30 ms step is AR wait caused by desynchronized per-socket clocks. Direct test = clock lock (needs AGT).

  (unverified): node/board-level power arbitration between sockets.
- This bench is the cheap A/B for the clock lock (expect cross-socket max-mean -> ~same-socket level when locked).
